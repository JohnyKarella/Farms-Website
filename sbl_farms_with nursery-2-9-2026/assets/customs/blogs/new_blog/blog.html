A Day in the Life: What Happens When 200 School Children Visit Our Farm

Every weekend, groups of curious young minds pour through our gates — and the farm comes alive in ways we never expected. From milking sessions at dawn to harvesting tomatoes they'll take home for dinner, discover how SBL Farms' Agritour program is quietly reshaping urban children's relationship with food, soil, and the natural world.

This particular Saturday began at 6:30 AM when the first bus rolled in carrying 48 students from Indiranagar. Within minutes, the silence of the farm had transformed into laughter, questions, and the irresistible pull of mud. Our head farmer, Ravi, guided them through the paddy fields while our dairy team set up the milking demonstration that never fails to produce expressions of wide-eyed wonder.
