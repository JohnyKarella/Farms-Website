Enhanced Agri Tour Experience at SBL Farms

🌿 A Living Farm Where Something Is Always Growing

What makes an agri tour at SBL Farms truly special is that this is not a static showcase — it’s a living, evolving ecosystem.

    As you walk through the farm, you’re surrounded by:
  • Mature arecanut plantations that have been thriving for over a decade. Coconut groves with thousands of palms, representing multiple traditional and high-yield varieties.
  • Intercropping zones where nutmeg, pepper, and flowering plants grow together — just as nature intended.
  • Visitors don’t just see crops here — they understand how farms are designed to be productive, resilient, and sustainable.


🍍 Exotic Fruit Trails You Rarely See in One Place

One of the most surprising parts of the agri tour is the exotic fruit section.

  • Here, visitors come across fruit varieties that most people have only heard of — or never seen at all. From cherry guava and mulberry to kumquat orange, rose apple, hog plum, white sapota, and even durian, the farm preserves a diverse collection of fruit trees.
  • Seasonally, guests also walk through large mango orchards featuring well-known and specialty varieties, making the farm visit feel like a journey across India’s fruit heritage.
  • This is often a favourite zone for families and children — curious, colourful, and memorable.


🌱 One of Karnataka’s Largest Plant Nurseries – Open to Visitors

A major highlight of the agri tour is the massive nursery at SBL Farms.

    SBL Farms is:
  • One of the largest suppliers of plants to Bangalore.
  • Among the largest producers of select cut plants in Karnataka.

    • Because plants come directly from the source, visitors can:
  • Explore indoor, outdoor, landscaping, medicinal, and aromatic plants.
  • Buy healthy plants at significantly lower prices than retail markets.
  • For many visitors, this alone makes the trip worthwhile.

🐄 Animal Life & Organic Farming Cycles

The agri tour also introduces visitors to ethical animal husbandry practices.

    Cows, sheep, and farm animals are cared for as part of a closed-loop system, where:
  • Animal waste is converted into organic manure.
  • Vermicompost and natural inputs improve soil fertility.
  • Farm waste supports biogas and energy needs.
  • This helps visitors understand how real farms reduce waste and stay productive year after year.

Diverse Farm Verticals Under One Ecosystem

01 Agri Tours

Agri Tours

Agri tours at SBL Farms are designed to bridge the gap between people and the source of their food. These guided farm visits allow visitors to explore crops, soil practices, irrigation systems, and sustainable farming techniques in a real-world setting.
Ideal for schools, families, and institutions, our agri tours focus on experiential learning rather than observation. Visitors gain insights into seasonal farming, organic inputs, composting, and water conservation — making it one of the most enriching farm experiences near Bangalore.

02 Dairy

Dairy

The dairy unit at SBL Farms follows ethical and hygienic practices focused on animal welfare. Visitors learn about cattle nutrition, milking processes, and how responsible dairy farming supports sustainability.
For children and families, this becomes an engaging introduction to where milk truly comes from, making SBL Farms a trusted animal farm in Bangalore for educational visits.

03 Green Wall

Green Wall

Our green wall vertical combines aesthetics with environmental impact. Using carefully selected plant species, SBL Farms designs and maintains green walls that improve air quality, regulate temperature, and enhance visual appeal.
This vertical supports architects, corporates, and homeowners looking for nature-inspired landscaping solutions in Karnataka.

04 Aquatic Plants

Aquatic Plants

SBL Farms cultivates a wide range of aquatic plants used for ponds, water bodies, and landscaping. These plants help maintain ecological balance, support aquatic life, and enhance water aesthetics.
Visitors gain awareness of aquatic biodiversity and water-based ecosystems through guided learning experiences.

05 Event Venue

Event Venue

From educational workshops to curated private events, SBL Farms offers a serene, green backdrop that transforms any gathering into a memorable experience. The open farm setting makes it ideal for eco-conscious celebrations and learning-based events.

06 Aviary

Aviary

The aviary at SBL Farms is designed as a natural habitat where birds thrive in spacious, well-maintained environments. This section is especially popular during farm visits near Bangalore, offering educational value for children and bird enthusiasts alike.

07 Landscaping

Landscaping

Our landscaping services blend functionality with natural beauty. Using native plants and sustainable techniques, SBL Farms creates outdoor spaces that are easy to maintain and environmentally responsible.

08 Corn Silage

Corn Silage

Corn silage produced at SBL Farms supports healthy livestock nutrition. This vertical focuses on quality, consistency, and long-term animal well-being.

09 Biogas

Biogas

The biogas unit at SBL Farms converts organic waste into clean energy, reducing dependency on fossil fuels and closing the loop on farm waste management.

01
📍 Where is SBL Farms located?

SBL Farms is located at Kolur, Gururayanapura Village, Ramohalli Post, Tavarekere, Uttarahalli Hobli, Bengaluru, Karnataka – 560074. Easily accessible from Bangalore, the farm is surrounded by greenery and offers a peaceful escape from the city. You can navigate directly using Google Maps for a smooth and hassle-free visit.

02
⏰ What are the visiting timings at SBL Farms?

SBL Farms is open for agri tours from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
A full-day visit gives you ample time to explore the farm, enjoy activities, relax in nature, and experience fresh farm food at an easy, unhurried pace.

03
🥗 What kind of food is served at SBL Farms?

We serve pure vegan meals prepared using organically grown produce from our own farm, along with nutritious food products from Sri Bhagyalakshmi Foods, backed by over 50 years of food legacy. The food is simple, fresh, wholesome, and perfectly aligned with a healthy, farm-to-plate lifestyle.
